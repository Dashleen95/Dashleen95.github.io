About

Dashleen Kaur

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia.

My research focuses on education, gender, and development, using administrative data and field experiments. My job market paper, with Alejandra Quintana, studies how the move from single-sex to coeducational schooling affects students’ test scores, university course selection, and earnings in Colombia.

Outside of research, I'm usually on a sunny walk with my dog Meera, drinking matcha, or at the pottery wheel.

I am on the 2026–27 job market.
