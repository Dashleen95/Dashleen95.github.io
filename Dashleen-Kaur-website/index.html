About

Dashleen Kaur

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the Vancouver School of Economics, University of British Columbia.

My research focuses on education, gender, and development, using administrative data and field experiments.

My job market paper, with Alejandra Quintana, studies how the move from single-sex to coeducational schooling affects students in Colombia. I also work on peer tutoring in rural India and women’s control over their earnings.

Outside of research, I enjoy spending time with my dog, painting the occasional mandala, gardening, and trying my hand at pottery.

I am on the 2026–27 job market.
